Q+A Katha - London 🧠

Question: If you were to code software in this language you'd only be able to type 0's and 1's.

Answer: Binary - Science: Computers. Itha kelvi London pathi - Answer Binary. Oru kaalathula oru oor la London nu oru periya vishayam irunthuchu, yaarum atha purinjikama irunthanga, oru chinna paiyan thaan London oda arumaiya purinjikittan, avan London pathi ellaar kitta yum pesi puriya vachan, mella mella oor makkal ellam London ah purinjikittanga, antha oor ye London nala azhaga maariduchu, ithula irunthu namakku theriyura vishayam enna na, London ah purinjikita life ye azhagahum.
